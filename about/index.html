About

I'm Simone Cammel. I designed the model and its assumptions and built the game with Claude.

The model has not been clinically validated. The town, its people and its care group are invented, and most of the game's numbers are mine, picked for play; How the model works says which ones and the Sources page lists the real figures behind the rest.

I also designed the Agent Reliability Lab under Experiments, a separate synthetic test of an AI model at a hospital referral desk.

I work in Big Tech, so read Cammelot with my bias towards AI in mind. Cammelot is my own project and is not connected to my employer.

Code and first drafts of the text were written with Claude under my direction. The figures on this site change when the code or the sources do.

Contact

Questions, corrections and criticism are welcome at contact@cammelot.org, especially if a number is wrong or a source doesn't say what I claim. Pictures and facts for writing about the game are in the press kit.
